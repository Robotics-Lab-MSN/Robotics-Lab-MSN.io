Alumni and where they go

This page collects public salary benchmarks from national surveys and graduates of ASU's Robotics and Autonomous Systems program. A "RAS graduate" card means the person graduated from the program; it does not by itself mean they worked in this lab.

Hands-on work at the lab's robot cells.

Public salary benchmarks

Independent benchmarks, all United States, in US dollars. Each comes from a different survey with a different cohort, year, and population, so they are not directly comparable with each other - and none of them measures outcomes of this lab or the Robotics and Autonomous Systems program specifically. They are reference points, not promises.

Benchmark Figure Population, cohort, year Source
Average starting base salary, engineering bachelor's degrees $78,731 US Class of 2025, projected starting offers, all engineering majors NACE Winter 2025 Salary Survey
Average starting base salary, engineering master's degrees $94,086 US Class of 2025, projected starting offers, all engineering majors NACE Winter 2025 Salary Survey
Median starting salary, engineering doctoral degrees $115,956 (middle 50%: $93,352-$131,000) US 2025 doctoral graduates, all engineering majors NACE 2025 doctoral-degree starting salaries
Median annual wage, "Engineers, All Other" - the BLS wage category O*NET reports for robotics engineers $122,930 US employed engineers in that broad BLS category, 2025 (not a starting salary, not robotics-specific) O*NET OnLine, Robotics Engineers (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data)
Median full-time salary, ASU engineering graduate degrees $95,000 ASU Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering graduate-degree alumni, recent cohorts ASU Fulton Schools graduate degree outcomes

Actual offers vary by role, industry, and location. These benchmarks describe broad populations - not this lab's students, and not any one degree's premium over another.
