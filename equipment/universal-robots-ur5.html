Universal Robots UR5

Collaborative robots · designed to work safely alongside people

What it is

The lab's lead robot arm: a collaborative robot designed to work safely alongside people. Collaborative robots ("cobots") need no safety cage for many tasks, which makes them well suited to a teaching room.

How the lab uses it

The UR5 appears in the industrial automation lab series: the collaborative robot and PLC integration lab (Industrial Automation Lab 5, in preparation) integrates a FANUC or Universal Robots platform with a PLC and validates an interlocked workcell sequence. Students also meet the UR5e, a sibling model, in simulation in the Isaac Sim lab series.
