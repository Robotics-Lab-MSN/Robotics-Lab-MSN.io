Dobot arms

Desktop robots · teaching platform for the articulated robot lab series

What they are

Compact desktop robot arms used for introductory robot programming instruction. The lab teaches on the Dobot Magician Lite, a small articulated arm that students program both with Blockly blocks and with Python.

How the lab uses them

The Dobot arms are the hardware platform for the articulated robot lab series in RAS 545 (Robotics Systems I). Across the four labs, students move the arm safely and record its coordinates, pick and place objects with suction-cup and gripper end effectors, detect and sort objects with a camera, and verify forward and inverse kinematics on the real robot.

Related instructional labs

  • Lab 1: Robot operation, coordinate recording, and drawing
  • Lab 2: Pick-and-place with suction and gripper end effectors
  • Lab 3: Object detection, classification, and sorting
  • Lab 4: Forward and inverse kinematics
