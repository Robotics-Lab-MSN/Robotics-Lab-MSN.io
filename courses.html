Courses and training in the lab

Taught in the lab

Graduate course

RAS 545 - Robotic Systems I

A core course of ASU's Robotics and Autonomous Systems master's program, taught by Prof. Sangram Redkar. Its guided laboratories run on this lab's Dobot arms, Isaac Sim workstations, and ROS 2 tooling - the full guides live on this site.

Industry training

PLC and automation training

Hands-on programmable logic controller and industrial automation training delivered by the lab's teaching team for industry partners, using the lab's Allen-Bradley PLC workbenches and robot cells.

From the RAS and manufacturing curriculum

The lab sits in the School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks, whose Robotics and Autonomous Systems and manufacturing programs include these related courses (program curriculum, per the school's graduate degrees page):

  • RAS 546 - Robotic Systems II
  • RAS 550 - Mechatronic Systems
  • RAS 557 - Foldable Robotics
  • RAS 585 - Machine Learning and AI
  • MFG 524 - Engineering Computing with Python
  • MFG 598 - Programming of Industrial Robotics System
  • MFG 598 - Additive Manufacturing
  • MFG 598 - CNC Computer Programming

The university's Robotics and Autonomous Systems courses page has the full program structure and elective lists.
