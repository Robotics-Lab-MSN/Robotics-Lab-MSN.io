Faculty

Faculty lead

Prof. Sangram Redkar

Professor and Associate Director in the School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks, working in robotics, dynamics and control systems. The lab and its instruction are built through his leadership, and he teaches RAS 545 Robotic Systems I, whose guided laboratory series lives on this site.

PhD researchers

PhD researcher

Sai Srinivas Tatwik Meesala

PhD candidate in Robotics and Autonomous Systems and Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Redkar Research Group, with his master's and bachelor's degrees in robotics from ASU. He develops and teaches the lab's hands-on robotics laboratories - ROS 2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, FANUC and Universal Robots arms, and PLC and SCADA systems - and co-authors the lab's articulated robot and Isaac Sim instructional lab series.
