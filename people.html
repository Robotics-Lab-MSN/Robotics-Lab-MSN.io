Faculty lead

Prof. Sangram Redkar

Professor and Associate Director in the School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks, working in robotics, dynamics and control systems. The lab and its instruction sit under his direction, and he teaches RAS 545 Robotic Systems I, whose guided laboratory series lives on this site.

Teaching assistant

Sai Srinivas Tatwik Meesala

Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Redkar Research Group and PhD candidate in Robotics and Autonomous Systems, with his master's and bachelor's degrees in robotics from ASU. He develops and teaches the lab's hands-on robotics laboratories - ROS 2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, FANUC and Universal Robots arms, and PLC and SCADA systems - and co-authors the lab's articulated robot and Isaac Sim instructional lab series.
