01 - About

What is this lab?

This site is the lab's public directory: the robots and equipment it has, the courses taught in it, the projects happening in it, and the resources it offers - for visitors, and for students thinking about the Robotics and Autonomous Systems major.

Everything here is written so anyone can follow along - no jargon, no gatekeeping.

05 - Isaac Sim labs

Learn robotics in simulation

Step-by-step lab guides that take a student from opening NVIDIA Isaac Sim to driving a simulated robot - written by the lab's own members for RAS 545.

Lab 1

First steps in Isaac Sim

Open Isaac Sim, build a small scene with lights and objects, and run your first physics simulation.

Do Lab 1 on this site →

Lab 2

Drive a UR5e robot arm

Control a six-joint UR5e arm in Isaac Sim - by hand, with inverse kinematics, and from Python.

Lab 3

TurtleBot 3 + ROS 2

Give a simulated TurtleBot 3 sensors, let it work out where it is, and set it navigating on its own.
